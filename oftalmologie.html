Oftalmologie Flashcards

How to use
  • Q&A cards: press Space or tap the card to flip it, then rate yourself: ← Known (card disappears), ↑ Fuzzy (comes back after 8 cards), → Don't know (comes back after 3 cards). Rating moves on to the next card.
  • Multiple choice (switch with the first button): a question can have more than one correct option. Tap options or press A to E to select, then Check (Enter). Only the exact set of correct options counts. Green = right, red = wrongly picked, green outline = missed. Press Next (Enter) to continue. Correct: it returns once more; answer it right twice in a row and it disappears. Wrong: it returns after 3 cards.
  • On a phone: use the coloured arrow buttons below the card, or swipe the card left (known), up (fuzzy) or right (don't know).
  • Corrections: an orange note means the answer in the source material was corrected or unclear, with the reason.
  • Overview & search shows every card by status and lets you search (accents optional, e.g. "cocka"). Tap a card to study it now, even if it is already known.
  • Progress is saved in this browser. Reset progress clears it.
